2  Getting an account

You cannot request your own ARCC account. A PI (UW faculty or research scientist) must request it against a specific project, in writing. No project means no account. Non-UW collaborators get restricted “ARCC-Only” accounts with real limitations. Sort this out first — it commonly blocks new performers for weeks.

2.1 How accounts work

ARCC will not create an account without written confirmation from a Principal Investigator who owns a project. Accounts are tied to funded projects for accounting and fair-share purposes.

So the first step is a conversation, not a form:

“Which project am I being added to, and can the PI email ARCC to add me?”

For ASCEND/ARID performers the project is something like cowy-firesurro (the project this guide was written under). Confirm the exact project name with your PI — you type it into every job you run (Chapter 10).

2.2 Who can be a PI

  • UW faculty (extended-term / tenure-track) or a Research Scientist.
  • Not adjunct, not emeritus, not a postdoc or student.

If your advisor doesn’t qualify, someone who does must sponsor the project. This matters for a multi-institution engine like ASCEND, where the leader of your sub-project might not be UW faculty.

2.3 UW accounts vs. ARCC-Only accounts

ASCEND spans UW, CSU, CU Boulder, Mines, NCAR, and NEON, so this distinction affects collaborators:

Full UW account ARCC-Only account
Who UW-affiliated people External collaborators without a UW identity
HPC (MedicineBow) ✅ ✅
GitLab ✅ ✅
Globus data transfer ✅ ❌ cannot use Globus
Other UW services ✅ ❌
ARCC-Only accounts and Globus

External collaborators on ARCC-Only accounts cannot use Globus, the recommended way to move large datasets (Chapter 6). They’ll need a guest collection set up for them, or a UW person to broker transfers. Plan for this early if your data lives at CSU or NCAR.

2.4 The free tier

Each researcher’s baseline allocation:

  • 100,000 CPU core-hours per year
  • 20,000 GPU-hours per year

That is plenty for getting started — 100,000 core-hours is roughly one 96-core node running flat out for six weeks. Larger allocations and investor projects exist beyond this. Core-hours are counted as (cores × wall-clock hours), a reason not to over-ask for resources (Chapter 11).

2.5 The practical checklist

  1. Confirm with your PI which project you’re joining and its exact name.
  2. Have the PI email arcc-help@uwyo.edu to request your account on that project. (You emailing them yourself won’t work — they need the PI’s confirmation.)
  3. Once ARCC provisions you, you’ll get a UW username. Your first login is Chapter 3.
  4. If you’re an external collaborator, flag it now and ask how you’ll move data, since Globus won’t be available to you.

No VPN is required to reach ARCC, on campus or off, so once your account exists you can log in from anywhere.
