22  Troubleshooting

A symptom-first index to the rest of the book.

22.1 Login and access

“Permission denied (publickey)” when I ssh — it worked before. Your SSH key almost certainly expired (they last one year). Regenerate it in the portal’s SSH Key Manager, unzip into ~/.ssh, chmod 0600 -R ~/.ssh (Chapter 3).

“Permission denied” on a brand-new setup. Check permissions: chmod 0600 -R ~/.ssh, and make sure you unzipped the key into ~/.ssh. Confirm you’re using medicinebow.arcc.uwyo.edu, not the dead southpass name.

I can’t get an account at all. Only a PI can request it, in writing, against a project. You cannot self-register (Chapter 2).

Globus won’t let me add the collection. If you’re an external collaborator on an ARCC-Only account, you can’t use Globus. You need a UW-side guest collection (Chapter 6).

22.2 Jobs won’t submit

sbatch: error: ... You have to specify an account. Add --account=cowy-firesurro (Chapter 10).

Job violates accounting/QOS policy (...) at submit time. Your request exceeds a QOS limit (every QOS is DenyOnLimit). Usually --time exceeds the QOS ceiling — e.g. 5 days under normal (max 3 d). Lower --time or switch QOS (Chapter 12). Check ceilings with sqos and showuserlimits.

Invalid qos specification. Your account can’t use that QOS. Yours are debug, fast, interactive, long, normal (sacctmgr -n show assoc user=$USER format=qos).

22.3 Jobs won’t start

Stuck in PENDING with reason Priority. The machine is just busy and you’re in line. Either wait, or pick a higher-priority (shorter) QOS — QOS dominates priority here (Chapter 14). Small/short jobs backfill sooner.

Stuck with reason Resources. You asked for a lot (many cores, much memory, a scarce GPU). Right-size (Chapter 11), or accept the wait. showjobreasons <id> explains in plain language.

GPU job pending for hours. H100/L40S are heavily contended (Chapter 13). Develop on mb-a30 instead, and check gpuavail/showpartitions for what’s actually free before submitting.

Pending with QOSMax... or AssocGrpCpuLimit. You (or your account) hit a cap — e.g. too many cores in flight at once. Wait for your other jobs to finish, or throttle an array with % (Chapter 16).

22.4 Jobs fail

OUT_OF_MEMORY. Raise --mem. Compare MaxRSS to ReqMem in sacct (Chapter 19). Watch the 10 GB/core default trap (Chapter 11).

TIMEOUT. Job outran --time. Checkpoint (Section 18.3), raise --time within the QOS ceiling, or split the work into an array.

FAILED, worked interactively but not in batch. You loaded modules / activated conda on the login node but not inside the submit script. Put them in the script (Chapter 10, Chapter 8).

Output file references files that “don’t exist.” Relative paths from the wrong directory, or you wrote to /lscratch (wiped at job end). Use absolute paths or $SLURM_SUBMIT_DIR; copy /lscratch output out before the job ends (Chapter 5).

PREEMPTED / REQUEUED on a long job. You were on investor hardware and got reclaimed. Move to --partition=non-investor and checkpoint (Chapter 18).

22.5 Software

“That module doesn’t exist” but I’m sure it does. module avail only shows what’s reachable now. Use module spider <name> — you probably need to load a compiler first (Chapter 7).

CUDA won’t build / link. Load gcc/13.2.0 (not 14) — cuda-toolkit is built against 13.2 despite its deprecation warning (Chapter 7).

Home directory full, everything breaking. Conda in /home. Move envs to /project/<proj>/software via ~/.condarc, and clean up (Chapter 8). Check with du -sh ~/* | sort -h.

GPU job runs but is slow / nvidia-smi shows 0%. Your code isn’t actually on the GPU. Verify the framework sees CUDA; in a container, you need --nv (Section 17.2).

22.6 Still stuck?

Email arcc-help@uwyo.edu with your job ID, the submit script, and the error. Or bring it to office hours, Tuesdays 11am–1pm on Zoom. Include what you already tried — it gets you a faster answer.
